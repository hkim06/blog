Welcome to my dev blog.

I’m a software engineer focused on building cloud-based voice and real-time communication systems. This blog is a place where I share practical lessons from designing, building, and operating VoIP platforms—covering topics like SIP, media, scalability, reliability, and the trade-offs you only discover in production.

My goal is to document what works, what doesn’t, and why—so others building similar systems can learn from real-world experience.
